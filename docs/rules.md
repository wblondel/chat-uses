# Stream rules (draft)

Drafts for the three places the rules appear (PLAN.md Phase 5): the
overlay's `!rules`, the YouTube description, and Discord's `#rules`. Same
rules everywhere, shorter where space is short. Edit them to your voice
before the first public stream; keep them in line with what the tools
really do (docs/phase5.md).

## One line (`!rules`, the ticker)

> Be decent: no slurs, hate, harassment, personal details or sexual content.
> Moderators can pause the stream, time out and ban. Full rules: Discord
> #rules.

## YouTube description

> **Chat controls this computer.** Type commands in chat (`!help`) and the
> machine does what chat says.
>
> **Rules**
> - No slurs, hate speech or harassment, in commands, names or on screen.
> - No personal details: yours or anyone's (names, addresses, phone numbers,
>   emails, links).
> - No sexual, violent or shocking content on screen.
> - Don't try to attack the stream, the server or other viewers.
>
> Text that breaks these rules is refused before it reaches the screen.
> Three refused slurs in 10 minutes earn a 10-minute timeout. Moderators can
> cover the stream at once, time out and ban, here and on Discord alike.
>
> What we keep and for how long: [privacy notice](LINK TO docs/privacy.md OR
> THE SITE).

## Discord `#rules`

> **Welcome. Chat controls a real computer, live on YouTube.**
>
> 1. **Be decent.** No slurs, hate speech, harassment or threats, here, in
>    YouTube chat, in names, or on the machine's screen.
> 2. **No personal details.** Not yours, not anyone's: real names,
>    addresses, phone numbers, emails, private links.
> 3. **Nothing sexual, violent or shocking** on screen, in messages or in
>    names.
> 4. **Don't attack the stream or the server.** Breaking the machine is the
>    game; going after the infrastructure or other people isn't.
> 5. **Moderators decide.** They can cover the stream (`/panic`), time out
>    and ban across YouTube and Discord. If you think a decision was wrong,
>    message a moderator calmly, not in `#control`.
>
> **How it works:** commands go in `#control` (or YouTube chat): `!help`
> lists them. The control panel's buttons in `#control` vote and push the
> meter, as typing `!yes` or `!democracy` does. Each run of the machine has
> 3 lives: a rewind costs one, and when the machine dies chat votes
> `!revive` (a life) or `!rip`. Its card names the last command before the
> end: a joke, not an accusation. Refused text never reaches the screen; three refused slurs in
> 10 minutes are a 10-minute timeout. Moderators get the screen's text read
> to them (screen OCR) and can stop everything in a second.
>
> **Your data:** see the privacy notice (LINK). Ask a moderator, or write to
> the contact address there, to have your data deleted.
