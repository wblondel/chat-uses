# Stream rules (draft)

Drafts for the places the rules appear (PLAN.md Phase 5): the overlay's
`!rules`, the YouTube description, Discord's `#rules` and the public site. Same
rules everywhere, shorter where space is short. Edit them to your voice
before the first public stream; keep them in line with what the tools
really do (docs/phase5.md).

## One line (`!rules`, the ticker)

> Be decent: no slurs, hate, harassment, personal details or sexual content.
> Moderators can pause the stream, time out and ban. Full rules: Discord
> #rules.

## YouTube description

> **Chat controls this computer.** Type commands in chat (`!help`) and the
> machine does what chat says.
>
> **Rules**
> - No slurs, hate speech or harassment, in commands, names or on screen.
> - No personal details: yours or anyone's (names, addresses, phone numbers,
>   emails, links).
> - No sexual, violent or shocking content on screen.
> - Don't try to attack the stream, the server or other viewers.
>
> Text that breaks these rules is refused before it reaches the screen.
> Three refused slurs in 10 minutes earn a 10-minute timeout. Moderators can
> cover the stream at once, time out and ban, here and on Discord alike.
>
> **By taking part you accept our rules and privacy notice:** SITE/rules.html
> and SITE/privacy.html. This stream reads its chat with YouTube API
> Services: sending commands here also means you agree to be bound by the
> YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms); Google's
> Privacy Policy: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy. What YouTube
> gives us about you is kept 30 days at most.

(Replace SITE with the site's address. YouTube's API rules ask that players
can see the terms and the privacy notice before taking part: this paragraph,
the stream's notice bottom right, and the site's footer do that.)

## Discord `#rules` and the site

The full rules are `site/pages/rules.md`: the public site shows them
(`rules.html`), and the same text goes in Discord's `#rules` (Discord reads
its Markdown: leave out the title and the comment, and give the privacy
notice's link the site's address).
