# Privacy notice

**Who:** chat-uses is run by William Blondel, France. Contact:
[contact@127011.xyz](mailto:contact@127011.xyz).

**What this is:** a live stream where chat controls a computer. To run it,
we process what you send in the stream's chat on YouTube and in our Discord
server's `#control` channel. By sending commands, you agree to this notice
and to our [rules](rules.html).

## YouTube

chat-uses uses **YouTube API Services** to read the stream's YouTube chat.
If you take part from YouTube, you are also bound by the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's
handling of your data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

We read only the stream's public chat, with an API key: we never ask you to
sign in, and we can't see or change anything in your Google or YouTube
account. From YouTube we get, for each chat message: your channel ID, your
name and badges as chat shows them (owner, moderator, member, verified), the
message, and for a Super Chat its tier. We don't use YouTube's viewer
counts or statistics.

**We keep what YouTube gives us for 30 days at most** (YouTube's rules):

- Your channel ID, name and badges are kept while you keep playing. 30 days
  after your last message, they're deleted, with your points, your commands
  and your log entries, and your name is taken out of the runs' pages (it
  becomes "a YouTube viewer"). Play again later and you start afresh.
- Your commands and the log entries naming you are deleted 30 days after
  you sent them, even while you keep playing.
- If you're banned, your channel ID (without your name) is kept while the
  ban lasts so that it keeps working; we check with YouTube at least every
  30 days that the channel still exists, and delete it when it doesn't.
- Our Discord's `#graveyard` posts never name YouTube viewers.

## What we keep

- **Who you are on the platform:** your YouTube channel ID or Discord user
  ID, your display name as the platform shows it, when we first and last saw
  you send a command, and your roles there (moderator, member, regular).
- **Your commands:** each `!` command you send, when, and what became of it
  (run, refused, and why). Pressing a button on the Discord control panel
  counts as sending its command. Ordinary chat messages aren't stored.
- **Moderation records:** timeouts and bans (with who decided and why), and
  the moderation log (panics, lockdowns).
- **Screenshots of the stream's computer** when its text matched our list of
  slurs (screen OCR), for moderators to check.
- **Session statistics:** for each stream session, counts only (how many
  were active in chat, how many commands and votes), never who.
- **The Graveyard and the runs' pages:** when a run of the machine ends,
  its card keeps its last commands with the names of those who sent them
  (as the stream showed them), including "probably killed by", and the
  names of those who sent the most commands in it, with how many. They're
  the run's history, on this site and in our Discord's `#graveyard`. A
  YouTube viewer's name comes out after 30 days (above), anyone's on
  request.
- **Points and achievements:** your points, and which of a run's
  achievements you were credited with. The leaderboard shows the names
  with the most points. Points are this game's own, not YouTube data.
- **Super Chats:** that you sent one, and its tier (which picks the
  chaos it brings), never the amount nor its message.

What chat builds in the game (what's on the machine, save and macro names,
changed laws) is the game's, and stays: who proposed it doesn't (30 days
for YouTube viewers, on request for anyone).

We don't collect anything the platforms don't already show in chat: no
email, no IP address, no payment data. This site keeps no log of its
visitors, sets no cookies, runs no scripts and shows no ads; no third party
serves content here.

## Why

To run the game (who sent which command, rate limits, votes and points),
and to keep the stream safe (filtering, timeouts and bans, investigating
incidents). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running the stream and
keeping it safe (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

## How long

- **YouTube data:** 30 days at most, as above.
- **Discord data:** commands, the event log and screenshots for 90 days,
  then deleted automatically; your user ID, name and roles while you take
  part; leaderboards and other aggregates until you ask us to delete them.
- **Bans:** as long as the ban lasts.

## Who sees it

Our moderators, through private tools. What is public is what was public
already: the stream shows commands and display names as chat sent them
(names with slurs or personal details are shown as `viewer#1234`), and this
site shows the runs, the achievements and the leaderboard with the same
names. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else. Our server runs at
netcup GmbH (data centre: Nuremberg, Germany). YouTube and Discord process your data
under their own policies.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use:
write to [contact@127011.xyz](mailto:contact@127011.xyz) or ask a moderator on Discord, with your channel
or user ID. **We delete your data within 7 days** (a ban in force is kept,
without your name, so it can't be avoided this way). Deleting it here
deletes only our copy: it doesn't affect what YouTube or Discord store. To
delete your messages or data there, use YouTube or Discord themselves. You
can also complain to your data protection authority.
