**Welcome. Chat controls a real computer, live on YouTube.**

1. **Be decent.** No slurs, hate speech, harassment or threats, here, in
   YouTube chat, in names, or on the machine's screen.
2. **No personal details.** Not yours, not anyone's: real names,
   addresses, phone numbers, emails, private links.
3. **Nothing sexual, violent or shocking** on screen, in messages or in
   names.
4. **Don't attack the stream or the server.** Breaking the machine is the
   game; going after the infrastructure or other people isn't.
5. **Moderators decide.** They can cover the stream (`/panic`), time out
   and ban across YouTube and Discord. If you think a decision was wrong,
   message a moderator calmly, not in `#control`.

**How it works:** commands go in `#control` (or YouTube chat): `!help`
lists them. The control panel's buttons in `#control` vote and push the
meter, as typing `!yes` or `!democracy` does. Each run of the machine has
3 lives: a rewind costs one, and when the machine dies chat votes
`!revive` (a life) or `!rip`. Its card names the last command before the
end: a joke, not an accusation. Achievements (getting Doom running, say)
give points to everyone who played in the 10 minutes before, and some give
a life; `/me` on Discord tells you your points. Refused text never reaches
the screen; three refused slurs in 10 minutes are a 10-minute timeout.
Moderators get the screen's text read to them (screen OCR) and can stop
everything in a second.

**Your data:** see the [privacy notice](privacy.html). Ask a moderator, or write to
the contact address there, to have your data deleted.
